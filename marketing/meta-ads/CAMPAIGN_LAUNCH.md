# Sunny Stories — Meta Ads Launch Campaign

**Platform:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram)  
**Goal:** Find audiences who respond to the product, drive $2.99 taster purchases, build retargeting pool  
**Entry product:** $2.99 single story (lowest barrier for cold traffic)  
**Live site:** sunnystories.co

---

## Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1 — Cold Traffic (Top of Funnel)
**Objective:** Traffic or Conversions (start with Traffic to gather data, switch to Conversions once 50+ events)  
**Daily budget:** $20–30/day  
**Audience:** Cold (no prior interaction with brand)

| Ad Set | Targeting | Budget split |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| A — Parent interests | Parents 25–44, AU, interest: parenting, children's books, audiobooks, early childhood education | 40% |
| B — Travel + family | Parents 25–44, AU, interest: family travel, Australian travel, road trips with kids | 30% |
| C — Yoto specific | Parents 28–44, AU, interest: Yoto (if available), screen-free toys, Montessori | 30% |

### Campaign 2 — Retargeting (Bottom of Funnel)
**Objective:** Conversions (purchase)  
**Daily budget:** $10–15/day  
**Audience:** Warm (prior interaction)

| Ad Set | Audience | Budget split |
|--------|----------|--------------|
| A — Site visitors | Website visitors last 30 days, excluding purchasers | 50% |
| B — Video engagers | Watched 50%+ of founder video | 30% |
| C — IG engagers | Instagram profile visitors last 60 days | 20% |

### Exclusions
- Already purchased (create custom audience from customer email list once available)

## Entry Offer Strategy

**Cold traffic always leads with $2.99 — never $25 or $9.99/mo.**

The goal of a cold traffic ad is not to sell a $25 product to a stranger. It's to get them to spend $2.99, hear their child's name in a story, experience the magic, and upgrade naturally.

$2.99 → experience → $25 collection → $9.99/mo passport

## Testing Framework

**Week 1–2:** Run Founder Video vs Education Angle on same cold audience  
**Week 3:** Double budget on winner, kill loser, introduce new creative variation  
**Week 4:** If CPA (cost per purchase) is under $15 for $2.99 conversion, scale  

**Key metrics to track:**
- CTR (click-through rate) — aim for 1%+ on cold traffic
- Cost per landing page view — aim for under $0.80
- Cost per $2.99 purchase — aim for under $8
- View-to-purchase rate from retargeting — aim for 3%+

**Don't touch campaigns for the first 7 days** — Meta's algorithm needs data. Early interference resets the learning phase.
