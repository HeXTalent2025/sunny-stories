# Sunny Stories — Instagram Content Strategy

## The Content Engine Concept

Each post is built around a real Australian landmark already in the Sunny Stories location database. The format: a short Reel combining beautiful location imagery with a 30-second audio clip from a personalised story set at that location, with text overlays and a CTA.

**Why this works:**
- Location-tagged content gets organic discovery from people searching those places
- Audio story clips are the best possible product demo — parents hear quality immediately
- Educational angle drives saves and shares (high-value signals for the algorithm)
- Infinite content engine: 113+ locations, endless combinations

---

## Reel Format Template

**Duration:** 15–30 seconds  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080 x 1920px)  
**Structure:**
1. **0–3s Hook:** Bold text overlay on location image — stop the scroll
2. **3–20s Story clip:** Location imagery + narration audio plays
3. **20–28s CTA:** "Create a story set here for YOUR child" + app screenshot flash
4. **28–30s End card:** Sunny Stories logo + sunnystories.co

**Text overlay style:** White Fraunces-style serif on dark gradient overlay  
**Music:** Keep audio story as primary audio — no background music competing  

---

## Image Sourcing

For each location, source 3–5 high-quality images from:
- **Unsplash** (free, commercial use) — search "[location name] Australia"
- **Pexels** (free, commercial use)
- **Tourism Australia media hub** (media.australia.com — free for media use)
- **State tourism boards** (Tourism Victoria, Destination NSW, etc.)
- Your own family photos if applicable

---

## Audio Story Generation

For each post, generate a story through your own platform using the parameters listed in each brief. Export via the Download MP3 button. Use the 30-second segment noted in each brief.

**Generation tip:** Use your own children's names for the sample stories — adds authenticity if you share them organically, and you know the output will feel genuine.
