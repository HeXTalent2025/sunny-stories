Common questions

Everything you need to know

🛡️Is the content safe for my child?
Yes — and we take this seriously. Every story is generated by an AI model that we've specifically instructed to produce G-rated, family-friendly content only. Before any story is written, we automatically screen your inputs (names, interests, locations) for anything unsuitable. The story generator itself is given strict rules: if anything looks out of place, it silently substitutes it with something wholesome — so you always get a safe, warm story. Our platform is built for families with children aged 4–10, and every decision we make reflects that.
🎂What ages are the stories suitable for?
Stories are calibrated to each child's age. Ages 4–5 get short, repetitive, sensory-rich sentences with a safe and magical world. Ages 6–7 get slightly more plot and a little gentle challenge. Ages 8–10 get richer vocabulary, real emotional depth, and genuine Australian facts woven naturally into the narrative. If you have children of different ages, each story is tailored individually to whoever it stars.
⚡How quickly will I get my stories?
Fast. A single story ($2.99) is ready in under 1 minute. A full collection of 10 ($25) takes 2–3 minutes. Your first Story Passport story ($9.99/mo) is generated immediately after subscribing. Stories generate while you watch a live progress feed, and a magic link is emailed to you the moment they're ready — no account needed, just tap to listen.
🗺️What is the Australia Story Passport?
Our subscription — $9.99/month — delivers one brand new personalised audio story every Monday. Each story visits a new Australian location, building a growing passport of adventures across the country. We start with your family's favourite places, then each week introduce your child to a new Australian landmark — Uluru, the Great Barrier Reef, Cradle Mountain — with real Australian facts woven naturally into the narrative. After one year: 52 stories, 52 places explored. Cancel any time, and every story stays yours forever.
🎁Can I give these as a gift?
Absolutely — gifting is a big part of what we do. Choose your plan (single story, full collection, or a Story Passport subscription), add the recipient's name and select an occasion — Mother's Day, birthday, Christmas, just because — and we create a beautiful personalised gift reveal page they open themselves. You can even gift a subscription so their collection grows every week. The gift link is included in your confirmation email to share however you like. See how gifting works →
🔗How do I access my stories later?
Your stories are saved for 12 months from purchase. Every time you want to listen, just use the magic link in your confirmation email — no login, no password. We strongly recommend saving that email. You can also download each story as an mp3 so it's yours forever, even after the link expires.
✏️Can I customise what goes into the stories?
More than you might expect. You tell us your child's name, age, what they love, favourite Australian locations, and the vibe you're after. You can also add friends, cousins, or siblings as characters. Before payment, we show you a full Story DNA preview — and you can tap any location, activity, narrative arc, or character to swap it before a single word is written.
📻Can I use these on a Yoto player?
Yes — and it works brilliantly. Once your stories are generated, use the Download MP3s button to save them. Then upload to your Yoto player using Yoto's Make Your Own card feature at yotoplay.com/make-your-own. Your child gets fully screen-free, on-demand access to their own personalised stories — completely independently. Story Passport subscribers can load their growing collection onto a card as it builds each week.
🔒What do you do with my information?
The details you enter (names, interests, locations) are used only to generate your stories and are stored securely. We don't sell, share, or use them for marketing. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details. You can read the full picture in our Privacy Policy.
🎙️What does the narration sound like?
Each story is narrated by a warm Australian voice using ElevenLabs, one of the world's leading AI audio platforms. The voice is expressive but consistent — not robotic, not over-the-top. We've tuned the settings specifically to keep the tone steady and soothing for children. You can preview what it sounds like on the sample stories page.
